Idiag M360 (Spinal Mouse®)

A hand-held device you glide along the patient's back from C7 to the top of the sacrum. It records the contour of the spine and sends the data by Bluetooth to your computer. Its software estimates regional and segmental angles of the thoracic and lumbar spine in the sagittal and frontal planes, in standing, flexion, extension and side-bending.

The Idiag M360 does not diagnose. It provides measurements for a qualified practitioner to interpret, and does not replace imaging.
