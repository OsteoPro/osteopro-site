Evidence

Published studies, including their limitations.

2015

Livanelioglu et al., European Spine Journal, 2015 — in 51 adolescents with idiopathic scoliosis, frontal-plane Spinal Mouse measures agreed strongly with X-ray Cobb angles (r = 0.69–0.93); the authors concluded it suits follow-up but cannot replace X-ray.

2008

Ripani et al., Journal of Sports Medicine and Physical Fitness, 2008 — in 26 healthy young adults, repeat measures were reliable, but agreement with X-ray was poor at most segments.

2020

Kraus et al., International Journal of Athletic Therapy and Training, 2020 — in 40 healthy, active adults, mobee med hip-flexion readings closely matched 2D video (r = 0.95–0.98), reading 2–3° higher on average; one movement tested only.
